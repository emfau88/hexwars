# HEXFRONT audio sources

The runtime files in this directory are compact gameplay derivatives imported from two audited project repositories. All source works listed below were published under CC0 1.0. Credits remain included for provenance even though attribution is not legally required by CC0.

| HEXFRONT files | Source work | Author | Source | License | Processing and provenance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ui-confirm.mp3`, `ui-navigate.mp3`, `ui-denied.mp3`, `ui-toggle.mp3` | Kenney Interface Sounds | Kenney | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Small coherent family trimmed and speed-shaped in RoosterRage commit `de393f9bb23ac4d6dc870b261993f20163f4bd2f` |
| `send.mp3` | Light wooshes | florianreichelt | [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/florianreichelt/sounds/447806/) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Short region accelerated, filtered and converted to mono in the same RoosterRage commit |
| `capture.mp3` | Gem Collect SFX | Author not preserved in local metadata | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/gem-collect-sfx) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | 190 ms excerpt, high-passed and mixed quietly in the same RoosterRage commit |
| `result-victory.ogg`, `result-defeat.ogg` | Retro Magic Sound Effects | NSFRL | [itch.io](https://nsfrl.itch.io/retro-magic-sound-effects) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Short result derivatives imported from KesselKrawall-3-D commit `a64054140d52fc66f58f410ae28676d611e3aed3` |

The remaining brief arrival tone is generated at runtime with the Web Audio API and contains no third-party asset.
